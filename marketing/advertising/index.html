Advertising & privacy

Advertising can help support the Free Starter. Paid Household accounts are ad-free when signed in, including eligible household members. Sign in to Harbourline so we can recognise your paid access.

Where ads can appear

We have prepared Google AdSense display placements on the homepage and journal pages. Advertising is currently disabled while Google account setup and site approval are pending. The planner does not contain advertising placements.

What is shared

Our account check shares only whether advertising is allowed. It does not send your income, expenses, balances, household details or budget contents to Google. If we cannot confirm eligibility, we keep advertising hidden.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may collect information such as IP addresses, browser information and identifiers, and use cookies or web beacons to deliver, measure and personalise ads according to your choices and applicable consent settings. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage personalisation in My Ad Center.

Your choices

Once ads are activated, Google's consent messages will offer the choices required for your region. Ad personalisation settings do not replace cookie consent. You can also manage cookies in your browser. A paid Household subscription removes advertising when you are signed in.
