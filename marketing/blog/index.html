First release announcement

Harbourline is now open for early access.

A calmer way to see what your household money needs to do next, from payday through to the bills, savings and goals ahead.

1 August 2026 Harbourline team 3 min read

Today we’re opening Harbourline to early-access households in Australia.

Most budgeting tools ask you to look back at what happened last month. Harbourline starts with the next payday. It brings your income, bills, savings, debt and goals into one view so you can answer the useful question: what is safe to spend next?

What’s in the first release

Build a household plan around the dates and rhythms you already use. Harbourline keeps the plan practical, visible and ready for the next decision.

A plan that starts with payday

Add the income that comes in, the commitments that need to be covered and the goals you want to keep moving. Harbourline uses the dates and frequencies you provide to show what each pay cycle needs to carry.

  • See upcoming income and bills in one cash-flow forecast.
  • Set aside money for savings and debt without losing sight of the week ahead.
  • Check the amount that remains safe to spend after planned commitments.
  • Use weekly, fortnightly, monthly and yearly rhythms that fit your household.

Keep the useful parts close

The first release also includes household sync, optional Google Calendar support and reports you can export for review or record keeping. You can keep the plan available across supported devices, then take a clear copy with you when you need one.

Harbourline is designed to make money conversations easier. You should be able to see the same picture, talk through the next choice and adjust the plan as real life changes.

What happens during early access

Early access gives us room to improve the product with real household feedback. We’ll watch the places where planning feels unclear, listen to what you need at payday and keep the product focused on the decisions that matter.

Create an account, confirm your email and review the introductory price before you continue. You stay in control of the plan and can cancel at any time.
