# ADR 0002: Planning guidance boundary

## Status

Accepted for Release 1 and subject to professional legal review before public
commercial launch.

## Decision

Harbourline will provide deterministic calculations, projections and
educational planning prompts. It will not provide recommendations about
specific financial products.

The product will be described as a money-planning assistant rather than a
financial adviser.

## Consequences

- Guidance must expose its assumptions.
- Product-specific recommendations are excluded.
- Open-ended AI advice is excluded from the first paid release.
- Future advice capabilities require a separate legal, licensing and
  compliance decision.
