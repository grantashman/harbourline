## Purpose

Harbourline helps households organise budgets, paydays, bills, savings, debt
and financial goals. This policy explains how Harbourline collects, uses,
stores and shares personal information when a person uses the website or app.

## Information Harbourline handles

Depending on how a customer uses Harbourline, this can include:

- name, email address and account identifiers
- sign-in and account-security information
- household name, invited household-member email addresses and access roles
- income, expense, debt, savings, goal, transaction and planning information
- subscription status, payment processor customer and subscription identifiers,
  and invoice information
- support requests, correspondence and technical information needed to keep the
  service secure and reliable

Harbourline does not collect bank passwords, full payment-card numbers,
government identifiers or financial-institution account credentials.

## Use and sharing

Harbourline uses information to create and secure accounts, provide household
planning and exports, manage subscriptions, provide support, investigate
security issues and meet legal obligations. It does not sell personal
information or use financial planning data for advertising.

The person who creates a household can invite other people to it. Household
members can see the household information made available to their role.

## Service providers and overseas hosting

Harbourline uses specialist providers for cloud infrastructure, authentication,
payments, email and service operations. Payment-card information is collected
by Harbourline's payment processor and is not stored by Harbourline.

At the time this draft was prepared, Harbourline's primary application database
is hosted in Northeast Asia (Tokyo). Information may therefore be stored or
processed outside Australia. The published policy will maintain an up-to-date
list of material service providers or a clear way to obtain that information.

## Browser storage, retention and security

Harbourline may use browser storage on a customer's device to keep the app
responsive and preserve the current working copy between sessions. Customers
should protect shared devices with their normal screen-lock and browser
security controls.

Information is kept for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service,
meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. Customers can
export their account information and request account deletion through the app
once household-ownership responsibilities have been resolved.

Harbourline uses access controls, authentication, encryption in transit and
technical safeguards designed to protect customer information. No online system
is completely secure. Customers should use a strong unique password and enable
an authenticator app where available.
